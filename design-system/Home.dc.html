How it works

1. Turn a tile
On your turn, flip one letter from the bank face-up. Everyone can see it.
2. Play a word
Spot a word in the upturned tiles? Type it. If it's real and the letters are there, it's yours.
3. Steal a word
Add letters to steal someone else's word. CAT + S = CAST, and it moves to your word list.
